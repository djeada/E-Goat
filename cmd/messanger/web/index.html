📡 How Connections Work

E-Goat uses multiple connection methods to ensure reliable communication:

🚀
WebRTC STUN (Best)

Direct peer-to-peer connection. Works on most home networks. Lowest latency.

🔄
WebRTC TURN (Fallback)

Relayed P2P via TURN server. Works through strict firewalls and symmetric NATs.

⚡
WebSocket

Real-time bidirectional. Works in most corporate networks.

🌐
HTTP Polling

Universal fallback. Works everywhere but has higher latency.

📡
LAN Broadcast

Local network only. Perfect for same-network devices.

🌍 Connecting Over the Internet

1

Port Forward: Forward ports 8080 (HTTP) and 9000 (WebSocket) on your router to this machine.

2

Set Public Base: Start E-Goat with -public-base="http://YOUR_PUBLIC_IP:8080" or set EGOAT_PUBLIC_BASE environment variable.

3

Share Link: Share the invite link with external users. They can connect directly to your public IP.

🎯 Choosing a Strategy

  • auto: Recommended. Tries all methods in priority order.
  • p2p-only: For low-latency direct connections (may fail on strict networks).
  • relay-safe: For corporate/restricted networks.
  • lan-only: For local network use only (no internet).
